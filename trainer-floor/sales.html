Before you start

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I bring everything from Trainerize?

Trainerize lets you export a client-list CSV and print workout/program content to PDF. Its help center says completed workout history and progress statistics cannot be exported. Trainer Floor can import the roster and reconstruct workout content from PDFs or records you provide, but it cannot recover data missing from those files. There is no direct Trainerize sync.

Read Trainerize's export guide ↗
Will GPT notes become actual saved workouts?

Yes. In completed-workout mode, identifiable workout entries become completed sessions with exercises, loads and reps in History after you review and save. Completion is assumed by your import choice. Unrecorded dates and numbers stay unknown; the importer does not make them up.

Do I have to replace my current coaching platform?

No. Try Trainer Floor for logging sessions in person while you keep the other tools you use for payments, nutrition, messaging or programming. Decide whether it earns a place in your workflow.

Does reserving my rate charge me?

No. A reservation records your place in the founding offer. You remain on Free until paid subscriptions become available and you choose to subscribe. Your clients never pay to be tracked.

Can I take my records with me?

Yes. Download a JSON backup from Settings. It includes your client records and workout history.
