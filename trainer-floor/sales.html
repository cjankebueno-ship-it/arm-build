Why the cohorts exist

  • Early trainers help shape the product, so they keep an earlier price.
  • When a cohort fills, new trainers move to the next published rate.
  • Every cohort gets the same Trainer Floor product.
  • Your monthly rate stays locked while your subscription remains active.
  • $99/month is the permanent standard-price cap.

No fake countdowns or hidden price jumps. The only scarcity is the published number of spots in each founder cohort.
